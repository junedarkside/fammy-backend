# Travel Industry Professional Guide

## B2B Travel Platform Overview

This guide explains travel industry concepts and how they apply to our B2B travel platform.

## Industry Terminology

### Key Players
- **Tour Operators**: Create and operate tour packages
- **Travel Agencies**: Sell tour packages to consumers
- **Wholesalers**: B2B distributors of tour packages
- **Suppliers**: Hotels, airlines, ground handlers

### Package Types
- **Land Only**: Tour without flights
- **Air Inclusive**: Tour with flights included
- **Seat-in-Coach**: Group tours with set departures
- **Free & Easy**: Independent travel arrangements

### Pricing Concepts
- **Net Rate**: Base price before commission
- **Gross Rate**: Final price including commission
- **Single Supplement**: Additional charge for single room
- **Commission**: Agency percentage on sales

## Business Models

### Standard Distribution Flow
```
Tour Operator → Wholesaler → Travel Agency → Consumer
```

### Commission Structure
- **Standard Commission**: 10-15% of gross price
- **Group Commission**: 15-20% for group bookings
- **Early Payment Bonus**: Additional 2-5%
- **Performance Incentives**: Volume-based bonuses

### Booking Process
1. **Option Hold**: 48-72 hour inventory hold
2. **Provisional Booking**: Temporary reservation
3. **Documentation**: Passenger details collection
4. **Payment**: Deposit and balance payments
5. **Confirmation**: Final booking confirmation
6. **Voucher**: Travel document issuance

## Data Management

### Tour Information
- **Program Details**: Itinerary, inclusions, exclusions
- **Pricing**: Per person rates by room type
- **Availability**: Seat inventory by departure date
- **Seasonality**: Peak/shoulder/low season pricing

### Departure Management
- **Cut-off Dates**: Final booking deadline
- **Minimum Departure**: Minimum passengers required
- **Group Sizes**: Maximum capacity limits
- **Waitlist**: Overflow booking management

### Country Normalization
- **ISO Codes**: Standard country identification
- **Destination Names**: Consistent naming conventions
- **Regional Grouping**: Countries by geographic regions
- **Travel Requirements**: Visa, passport, health info

## Industry Standards

### Data Exchange
- **API Integration**: RESTful APIs for real-time data
- **XML Standards**: Tourico, OTI protocols
- **Static Data**: Seasonal brochures and tariffs
- **Dynamic Updates**: Live availability and pricing

### Payment Processing
- **Payment Schedule**: Deposit and balance dates
- **Cancellation Policies**: Refund rules and deadlines
- **Amendment Fees**: Change charges and penalties
- **Currency Handling**: Multi-currency pricing

### Compliance Requirements
- **Financial Protection**: Bond and insurance requirements
- **Licensing**: Tour operator and travel agency licenses
- **Data Protection**: Customer data privacy (GDPR)
- **Industry Associations**: IATA, UFTAA standards
